How to raise a claim

If something is damaged or missing, act quickly and keep records.

  1. 01

    Report promptly

    Tell the crew at delivery and contact your branch as soon as possible.

  2. 02

    Record the damage

    Take photos and note the issue on the delivery receipt before signing.

  3. 03

    Submit documents

    Share the policy, invoice, inventory and photos with the claim form.

  4. 04

    Assessment

    The claim is reviewed under the terms of the cover arranged.

Prepare for the coverage conversation

An inventory and condition record give you a starting point when discussing cover and its terms.

  • Record condition

    Photograph high-value or fragile belongings before collection.

  • Ask about the terms

    Confirm who provides cover and which items, routes or events qualify.

  • Keep records together

    Save the policy, invoice, inventory and delivery acknowledgement.

Reduce the risk of damage

Good preparation protects your belongings — and makes any claim simpler.

  • Declare high-value itemsList them before packing day
  • Crate fragile piecesGlass, artwork and antiques
  • Photograph conditionBefore collection and at delivery
  • Keep valuables with youJewellery, cash and documents
  • Check before signingOpen-first cartons and fragile items
  • Note issues on the receiptIn writing, at handover
