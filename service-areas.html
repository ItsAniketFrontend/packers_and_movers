Local or intercity?

Two kinds of move, planned differently.

Within the city

Local shifting

Same-city moves between homes, apartments and offices — usually packed, moved and unloaded in a day.

  • Mini trucks and closed-body vehicles
  • Society, lift and parking coordination
  • Room-by-room unloading and placement
Local home shifting
City to city

Intercity moves

Door-to-door moves between cities, with pickup, transit and delivery planned around your dates.

  • Pickup and delivery windows agreed up front
  • Secure loading for long-distance roads
  • Car and bike transport on the same route

Hill stations and the North-East

Ooty, Shillong, Siliguri and Guwahati moves need smaller vehicles, weather-ready packing and flexible arrival windows.

  • Shuttle loading on narrow roads
  • Waterproof wrapping
  • Realistic transit windows
