Local or intercity?

Two kinds of move, planned differently.

Within the city

Local shifting

Same-city moves between homes, apartments and offices — usually packed, moved and unloaded in a day.

  • Mini trucks and closed-body vehicles
  • Society, lift and parking coordination
  • Room-by-room unloading and placement
Local home shifting
City to city

Intercity moves

Door-to-door moves between cities, with pickup, transit and delivery planned around your dates.

  • Pickup and delivery windows agreed up front
  • Secure loading for long-distance roads
  • Car and bike transport on the same route

How your move works

The same four steps in every city.

  1. 01

    Request a quote

    Share your pickup, destination, date and a rough inventory online, by phone or on WhatsApp.

  2. 02

    Survey & confirm

    Walk the team through your home or office in person or on a video call, then approve a written quote.

  3. 03

    Pack & load

    The crew wraps furniture, packs and labels cartons, and loads heavy items first with fragile cartons secured.

  4. 04

    Deliver & settle

    Cartons go to the right rooms, agreed furniture is reassembled and you check the inventory before signing off.
