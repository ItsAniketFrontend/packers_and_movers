The right materials, labelled for a faster unpack.

    Choose materials

    Agree cartons, wrapping and crates for each type of item.

  2. 02

    Pack & label

    Each carton is labelled with the room and contents.

    Inventory list

    A carton-by-carton list travels with your goods.

  4. 04

    Unpack & clear

    Cartons are opened in the right rooms and materials collected.

Packing materials for different items

Good packing starts with the right materials. Choose protection based on weight, surface finish and fragility.

  • Glass and mirrors

    Discuss edge guards, cushioning and crate options where needed.

  • Clothes and linens

    Group by room, wardrobe or priority for quick unpacking.

  • Electronics and cables

    Keep labelled cables together and flag sensitive equipment.

Packing materials we use

Chosen by weight, surface finish and fragility.

  • Corrugated cartons3- and 5-ply
  • Bubble wrap & foamGlass, electronics and polished wood
  • Stretch filmSofas, mattresses and upholstery
  • Corner & edge guardsMirrors, frames and tables
  • Wooden cratesFragile and high-value pieces
  • Waterproof sheetsMonsoon and coastal moves
