What to plan for a move in Delhi

Every city has its own access rules, weather and building types. Confirm these details with the crew before moving day in Delhi.

  • Goods vehicle timings

    Ask the crew about entry-time restrictions for trucks on your route and plan the loading slot around them.

  • Beat the afternoon heat

    Schedule loading for early morning and keep heat-sensitive items such as electronics out of direct sun.

  • Society and lift permissions

    Check move-in or move-out rules with your housing society and book the service lift for loading.
