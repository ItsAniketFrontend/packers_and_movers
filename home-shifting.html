How home shifting works

From survey to the first night in your new home.

  1. 01

    Survey & quote

    Walk through your rooms in person or on video and get a written, itemised quote.

  2. 02

    Packing day

    Furniture is wrapped and cartons are packed and labelled room by room.

  3. 03

    Loading & transport

    Heavy items go in first; fragile cartons are secured separately.

  4. 04

    Delivery & set-up

    Cartons are placed by room and agreed furniture is reassembled.

Prepare your home for moving day

Walk through your rooms with the crew. Agree what will travel, what needs special packing and what stays with you.

  • Make a first-night box

    Keep medication, chargers, toiletries and a change of clothes within reach.

  • Measure bulky furniture

    Check door widths, lifts and stairs before dismantling or loading.

  • Label by destination room

    Mark fragile and open-first cartons so unloading is easier.
