What happens on moving day?

From the first carton to the final room, three steps help keep your move organised.

  • Before the crew arrives

    Set aside essentials and confirm access, parking and lift reservations.

  • While we pack and load

    Walk through the inventory with the crew and flag fragile or high-priority items.

  • At your new address

    Direct cartons to each room and check the inventory before signing off.

Your move in four steps

A simple process, whether you’re moving across the street or across the country.

  1. 01

    Request a quote

    Share your pickup, destination, date and a rough inventory online, by phone or on WhatsApp.

  2. 02

    Survey & confirm

    Walk the team through your home or office in person or on a video call, then approve a written quote.

  3. 03

    Pack & load

    The crew wraps furniture, packs and labels cartons, and loads heavy items first with fragile cartons secured.

  4. 04

    Deliver & settle

    Cartons go to the right rooms, agreed furniture is reassembled and you check the inventory before signing off.
