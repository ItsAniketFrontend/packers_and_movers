Get closer to a written quote

A useful estimate starts with details about both properties, what you’re moving and any extras that need to be included.

  • List bulky items

    Mention large appliances, heavy furniture and fragile pieces.

  • Describe access

    Share floors, lift availability and distance from parking.

  • Confirm your scope

    Check whether packing, unpacking, storage or vehicle transport is included.

Ways to keep your moving cost down

Small decisions before moving day can make a real difference to your quote.

  • Declutter firstSell, donate or discard what you won’t use
  • Book earlyMonth-end and weekends fill up first
  • Choose a weekdayMid-week, mid-month dates are easier to schedule
  • Pack some items yourselfBooks, clothes and linen are easy wins
  • Share an accurate inventorySo the right vehicle is booked first time
  • Combine your vehicleShip your car or bike on the same route
