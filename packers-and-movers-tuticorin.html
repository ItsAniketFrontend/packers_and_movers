What to plan for a move in Tuticorin

Every city has its own access rules, weather and building types. Confirm these details with the crew before moving day in Tuticorin.

  • Protection from coastal humidity

    Moisture-resistant wrapping helps protect wooden furniture, metal fittings and appliances.

  • Employer transfer paperwork

    Ask for invoices and consignment notes in the format your employer needs for reimbursement.

  • Beat the afternoon heat

    Schedule loading for early morning and keep heat-sensitive items such as electronics out of direct sun.
